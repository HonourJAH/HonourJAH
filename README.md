# Hi, I'm Michael 👋

Entry-level data analyst building end-to-end SQL and Python projects on messy, real-world data. Electrical and Electronics Engineering graduate (Federal University of Technology Akure), based in Nigeria and open to remote roles.

## Featured projects

**[Online Retail Sales Analysis](https://github.com/HonourJAH/online-retail-sales-analysis)**: profiled and cleaned 541,910 retail transactions in MySQL, with every excluded row flagged by reason and every decision documented, then answered ten business questions on sales, returns, products, countries, customers, segmentation (RFM) and retention, and presented the results in a four-page Power BI dashboard.

**[Czech Bank Analysis](https://github.com/HonourJAH/czech-bank-analysis)**: profiled a real bank dataset (8 related tables, over a million transactions) with a 17-point decision log, then answered ten business questions on money flow, channels, regions, loan risk, cards, account segments and overdrafts.

**[Bank Marketing Analysis](https://github.com/HonourJAH/bank-marketing-analysis)**: profiled and cleaned 41,188 phone-campaign records from a Portuguese bank in Python (pandas) with a 10-point decision log, then answered ten business questions on customer age, job and education, calling effort, contact method, earlier campaigns, call length, the economic climate and the best and worst customer segments, and showed that the year of the campaign explained several patterns that looked clear overall.
